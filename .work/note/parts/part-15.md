[h2] Technique 8: Rewrite copy by hand

[p] The copy a model puts in your design doesn’t affect the visuals, but it may have the biggest impact on whether users perceive your design as tasteful or slop. We are bombarded by AI-generated text every day. It’s fatiguing to read and just doesn’t feel natural.

[p] Think of AI-generated copy the way designers think of “Lorem ipsum” text: it’s there to help you visualize the structure, but it’s a placeholder that needs to be rewritten. Make sure a human reads each line of copy and rephrases it in a consistent voice.

[p] Here’s the difference that process can make:

[FIGURE 25] path: assets/how-to-turn-your-ai-into-a-world/figure-25-3174cc8a-c197-4be5-991d-0834d05cfc42_1456x1949.png

[p] The human version is almost always shorter, simpler, and less eyeroll-inducing. When users see an AI-generated wall of text, they immediately want to skim past. Make the effort to write clear, intentional copy, and users will actually read what you have to say.
