Proposed Korean slide title: 3단계 디자인 프로세스

[p] This is a lesson I learned from managing human designers, before I was managing AI ones. For most of my career at Apple, I led an R&D team designing exploratory future AI products. Early on, our preconceived notions about how user interfaces should work limited our creativity and kept us returning to the same old ideas. Through rigor and new processes, we learned to stop re-creating what’s comfortable and instead look to the fringes of what’s possible, to generate something new. We became experts at polishing the little details to an Apple level of quality.

[p] Since my time at Apple, I’ve been working on applying that same process to my work with AI. In the past couple years, AI agents have become extremely capable. They can do in hours what used to take my team weeks. And with the right guidance, they can create designs that look completely unlike anything else.

[p] Loosely inspired by the Double Diamond design process, I’ve reimagined the design process for a team of AI agents instead of human designers:
    (link: “Double Diamond design process” → https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_Diamond_(design_process_model))

[list] - Discover new ideas beyond the average slop by exploring a variety of directions and creating bold, ambitious design briefs.
- Define an individual design identity by pushing AI beyond its familiar patterns and chaining models together to fully realize the design’s potential.
- Deliver a stunning final result by polishing away the sloppy rough edges and focusing on the key elements.

[p] By following these stages and applying the techniques within each one, you can create an incredible design remarkably quickly—and make people ask, “Why does AI create magic for you (and not me)?”
