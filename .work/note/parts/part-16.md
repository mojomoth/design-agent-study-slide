[h1] Use your AI’s full design potential

[p] Most AI-generated designs out there today are slop, but AI can be a very good designer when directed in the right way. As AI continues advancing, new models will unlock design possibilities that human designers can only dream of—if we’re able to steer them. Once AI becomes a part of every design process, the advantage will come from knowing how to get more out of it.

[p] With today’s AI models and tomorrow’s, this three-stage process will help you guide your AI agents from a blank slate to a distinctive design that taps into AI’s full potential:

[list] - Discover new ideas beyond the AI slop defaults.
- Define a design identity that’s individual to you and your product.
- Deliver a delightful final result by polishing away the AI-designed rough edges.

[p] Ultimately, “taste” and “slop” are in the eye of the beholder. Great design brings together elements like fonts, colors, and layouts to evoke a feeling, not just create a visual composition. If your product feels delightful to use, your users won’t care if it was designed by Claude in a day or a team of humans over months. Focus on the feeling, and your designs will always stand out—AI-generated or not.
